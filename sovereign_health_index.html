AiTHOS · Sovereign Health

Sovereign Health

A new way to organize health care around one idea: the patient is the principal. Your health story belongs to you — and a trusted system helps you understand it and act on it.

The patient is the protagonist. AiTHOS is the architect.

The idea

Most health systems are built around institutions. Sovereign Health is built around you.

When you face a complex diagnosis, your information is scattered, the decisions are overwhelming, and no one is clearly on your side. Sovereign Health reorganizes the picture so that you — the patient — are the center, with a trusted guide and clear information to navigate what comes next.

You are the principal

Everything is organized to serve you — not an institution, insurer, or product. Your interests come first.

Inform, never direct

Sovereign Health helps you and your doctors see clearly. It never replaces medical judgment or makes decisions for you.

Time matters

Complex disease moves on its own clock. Understanding where you are — and what's changing — helps you act in time.

How it fits together

One system, built in layers

Sovereign Health has six parts. Two are active today; the others emerge over time, only as the system earns trust. Everything orbits the same center — the patient.

The six components, in plain terms

Each part has a job. Together they form a system that keeps the patient at the center.

01 · THE FOUNDATION

Sovereign Health

The whole idea

The overarching principle and system: health care organized around the patient as the sovereign decision-maker. Everything else exists to make this real.

Think of it as the constitution — the founding principle the rest of the system is built to uphold.

02 · THE INTELLIGENCE

AiTHOS & SAiRA

Active

AiTHOS is the intelligence layer that gathers your scattered records and turns them into one clear health story. SAiRA is the agent that does the work — assembling, organizing, and rendering your personal health summary.

Like a brilliant research assistant who reads everything in your file and hands you a clear, organized briefing before every appointment.

03 · THE GUARDIAN

GAiSHA

Active

The safeguarding layer — the governance and oversight that keeps the system trustworthy. It ensures the intelligence is used carefully, that limits are respected, and that the patient's interests stay protected.

The guardrails and the conscience of the system — making sure powerful tools stay in service of the patient.

04 · THE PROMISE

Health Fiduciary Contract

Active

The formal commitment that binds it all together: a duty to act in the patient's interest. It's what makes "the patient comes first" a real, accountable promise rather than a slogan.

Like the fiduciary duty your financial advisor owes you — a legal obligation to put your interests first, applied to your health.

05 · THE NETWORK

Sovereign Health Network

Future-state

A future connection layer that links patients, care teams, and trusted partners around the patient — so the right people can collaborate, with the patient's permission and always in the patient's interest.

Emerging over time, only once the foundation has earned trust. Built later, not asserted now.

06 · THE ECONOMY

Sovereign Health Fund

Future-state

A future mechanism intended to return value created within the system back to the community that generated it — so patients share in the value their participation helps create.

A concept for later phases, to be defined carefully with appropriate oversight. Not an investment offering.

Why it matters

One clear story

Your records, results, and history brought together into a single, understandable picture — instead of scattered across systems.

A real advocate

A trusted guide whose job is to represent your interests, so you're never navigating the hardest decisions alone.

Better conversations

Built on trust

The vision

Health care that answers to you.

Sovereign Health begins with a simple shift: make the patient the principal, and build everything else to serve that. The foundation is being proven today — one patient, one clear story, at a time.

Value returns to the principal.

Sovereign Health and AiTHOS provide informational support to help patients organize and understand their health information. They do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and do not replace physicians or care teams.
